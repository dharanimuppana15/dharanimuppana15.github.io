THE BANDWIDTH EGRESS TRADE

Ciena Corporation: The AI Trade’s Second Act & The Bandwidth Egress Paradox

“AI traffic doesn't monetize until it leaves the data center. Ciena moves those bits—and 3 of 4 major hyperscalers selected its architecture. But at ~80x forward earnings, the math says wait.”

The AI infrastructure trade has been almost entirely about what happens inside the data center—GPUs, HBM, power, substations, and cooling racks. But as compute scales and deployment pivots from training to recurring inference flowing to billions of users, the binding constraint shifts to the optical transport layer connecting geographically distributed campuses, regional hubs, and edge users.

Ciena delivered record FY25 results ($4.77B revenue, +19%), guided FY26 to 24% growth, and hyperscalers now account for ~38% of total revenue. But with CIEN trading near ~$503 at ~80x forward earnings, market expectations leave no margin for execution delays or hyperscaler capex pauses.
